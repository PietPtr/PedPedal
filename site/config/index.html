Updating the firmware

  1. Hold the BOOTSEL button on the Pico while plugging it in.
  2. Press Find Pico and pick the RP2 Boot device. After the first time, this page spots the Pico by itself.
  3. Press Flash firmware. Saved pedal settings are kept.

Flashing from the page needs Chrome or Edge.

Or press Download firmware and copy the .uf2 file onto the RPI-RP2 drive that appears when the Pico starts with BOOTSEL held.
