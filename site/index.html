MIDI Pedals Your Way

Control your DAW, synths, or virtual pedals with physical pedal switches over MIDI.

  • Momentary and Latching mode
  • RGB LEDs

Open Everything

Fully open PCB designs, firmware, driver, and parametric case designs. Simple to source components and easy to assemble, or have a PCB manufacturer assemble the boards for you if you'd like many boards.

  • KiCad boards
  • Rust firmware on a Raspberry Pi Pico
  • Through-hole or factory-assembled designs
  • Configuration over MIDI: via the browser or your code

Expandable & Parametric

One simple PCB design implements both the main board the daughter-boards for theoretically any amount of switches. The case design is parametric and can be generated to fit.

  • Any amount of switches
  • Chainable modules

Build it Your Way

Choose your own PCB color, laser-cut acrylic type, and optionally 3D printed sides to make it your own.

  • Acrylic top and bottom plate
  • Optional 3D-printed modular side panels
